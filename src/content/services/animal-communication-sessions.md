We offer animal communication sessions to support animals in the care of sanctuaries, rescues, and animal centres — anywhere animals in need are being looked after.

Every animal we connect with is met as a whole being: not something to fix or manage, but a fellow member of the same collective we're all part of. Communication and energetic support can offer insight into how an animal is really feeling, what might be troubling them, and what would genuinely help — information that's easy to miss when we can only observe behaviour from the outside.

## What this looks like

Sessions can focus on whatever the animal and the people caring for them need most, including:

- Understanding the root of a behavioural or emotional concern
- Easing the transition into a new environment after rescue
- Supporting animals through illness, aging, or end-of-life
- Processing grief, whether the animal's own or a companion's passing
- Simply checking in — how is this animal really doing, in their own words

## Who this is for

This service is offered specifically to organisations caring for animals in need: sanctuaries, rescues, and animal centres. If you care for animals and sense there's more going on than you can see from the outside, we'd love to help.
