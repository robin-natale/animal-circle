Animal Circle exists to support animals who cannot speak for themselves in the language we're used to, but who communicate constantly in their own way. We offer animal communication and energetic support to sanctuaries, rescues, and animal centres caring for animals in need, so that the animals in their care are seen, heard, and supported as fully as possible.

We hold a simple belief at the centre of this work: animals are not separate from us. They are part of the same collective, the same web of life, feeling and experiencing the world alongside us rather than beneath us. Every session, every conversation, and every piece of support we offer comes from that place.

- **Compassionate** — animal welfare guides everything we do
- **Grounded** — practical support for the people and organisations caring for animals every day
- **Connected** — recognising animals as kin, not as separate from human experience
