# Welcome to Animal Circle

We started Animal Circle from a simple belief: animals are not separate from us. They are part of the same collective, feeling and experiencing the world alongside us, deserving of the same compassion, care, and attention we'd give to anyone we love.

Our work is offered to sanctuaries, rescues, and animal centres caring for animals in need. Through animal communication and gentle energetic support, we try to help the people doing that daily care understand a little more about how the animals in their charge are really feeling — and what might help.

This blog will share reflections from that work, along with resources for the organisations we support. We're glad you're here.
